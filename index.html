About Me

I am a final year PhD candidate at USYD, supervised by Prof. Qiang Tang. Before moving to USYD, I have had 2-year PhD study at New Jersey Institute of Technology under Prof. Qiang Tang's supervision. Prior to that, I got my master's degree from Behang University and bachelor's degree from Shandong University.

Research Interests

My research interests mainly focus on applied cryptography in pratical usage, including:

  • End-to-Enc encrypted cloud storage, messaging, and online collaboration
  • Updatable encryption
  • Privacy protection and compliance of cryptocurrency exchange
