About Me

I am a final year PhD candidate at USYD, supervised by Prof. Qiang Tang. Before moving to USYD, I have had 2-year PhD study at New Jersey Institute of Technology under Prof. Qiang Tang's supervision. Prior to that, I got my master's degree from Beihang University and my bachelor's degree from Shandong University.

Research Interests

My research interests primarily focus on cryptography for real-world systems, particularly the design and formal analysis of end-to-end encrypted cloud services and privacy-preserving yet compliant cryptocurrency exchanges.
